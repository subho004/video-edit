# Learned preferences (append one generic line when a user states a durable preference; no names, places or trip details)

- Original look beats "improved" looks; a graded/tone-mapped version was rejected as over-exposed and over-filtered.
- Silent output; music is added in the app.
- A single POV-style text line, or none; never per-shot labels.
- Alternate back / POV walking shots with matching direction; no turn-to-camera endings; walking at 1x.
- Do not centre a reel on one subject; show the place.
- Approved parts stay byte-identical; changes are small and surgical.
- "Moving clip" means high-motion; state the interpretation of shorthand and allow a one-edit swap.
- Remove clips the user calls unrelated, near-duplicate or personal; shorter is usually better after the first cut (cut to ~30 s on request).
- Output saved in the footage folder itself.
- Always export 4K at the highest source frame rate and best quality; previews are only for review.
