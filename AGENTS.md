# AGENTS.md

This repo edits videos (reels, product videos) from raw footage. **Use the `video-edit` skill**: `.agents/skills/video-edit/SKILL.md`
(workflow, decision gates, scripts, references). Read it before touching footage. `README.md` has setup.

Always-on rules: exports are always 4K at the highest source fps and best quality; never modify or delete original footage; ask the user at the decision gates instead of guessing; generated files go in
`<footage folder>/_work/`; keep place/person names out of docs; secrets stay in `.env` (see `.env.sample`).
